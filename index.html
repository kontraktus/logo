Working features

Private contract intelligence

PDF, DOCX, and TXT uploads are extracted, chunked, embedded, and indexed locally. Answers cite checked source text; missing clauses and risks are evaluated against the knowledge base.

Human review

Reviewers can accept, reject, or adjust findings, add source-backed findings, record notes, and approve contracts or request changes. Analysis can be retried or cancelled while previous completed results are preserved.

Controlled exports

PDF reports include accepted findings, source evidence, reviewer notes, and the final decision. Upload, analysis, review, and export actions check current account permissions.
